Typography, spacing and layout stay identical to the original HowItWorks page. Only the paint moves: dark OLED palette, the signature amber-to-orange gradient on primary CTAs, the multi-stop blue-lavender-orange VIP gradient, plus shimmer sweeps, arrow nudges and the VIP glow aura.

4 · Animations imported from Home

What moves now in HowItWorks

Shimmer sweep on hover

A diagonal white-25% highlight crosses the button in 700ms with cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1). Applies to .btn-primary and .btn-vip.

Arrow nudge

.btn-arrow inside CTAs translates 3px to the right on hover (260ms spring). Subtle but every CTA in the product does it consistently.

VIP halo breathing

The .glass-vip card pulses its outer shadow opacity from 30% to 45% on a 4s infinite ease loop. Reduced-motion users see it static.

fade-up on scroll

Section headers and grids enter with opacity + translateY(24px) + blur(6px) resolving over 800ms when they hit the viewport (IntersectionObserver).

Typography, layout and spacing intentionally not shown. Out of scope.
