For Providers

Publish your listing in 5 steps

From registration to going live — and upgrading to VIP when you're ready.

1

Register

Create your account with email and a strong password. Free to start.

2

Verify

Confirm your phone number, upload a government-issued ID or passport, and link your social media profiles.

3

Create listing

Pick a category, write your description, set pricing, and upload photos. Edits always reset status to Pending.

4

The General Manager reviews your listing and photos, then approves, requests changes, or rejects with feedback.

5

Go live

Your listing is now public. Upgrade to VIP for higher ranking and a verified badge that builds customer trust.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

After registering, you confirm your phone number via SMS. Then you upload a government-issued ID or passport and link at least one social media profile. A General Manager reviews the documents and approves your Verified status. You cannot become VIP without completing all three verification steps.

The General Manager typically reviews listings within 24–48 hours. You will receive an email or WhatsApp notification when your listing is approved, rejected, or flagged for changes. Listings do not expire while awaiting approval.

The GM will send you feedback explaining the reason. You can edit your listing based on that feedback and resubmit. Every time you edit a listing — even after approval — it returns to Pending status for re-review before going live again.

Only customers who contacted a provider via the platform's WhatsApp button are eligible to leave a rating. This prevents fake or malicious reviews. All submitted ratings are reviewed by the GM before they appear publicly on the listing.

VIP pricing is set by the General Manager and may vary by category or season. Contact us via WhatsApp to get current rates. You must be Verified before applying for VIP. Regular listing is always free.
